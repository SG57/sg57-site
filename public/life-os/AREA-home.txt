# Home
`version: 2.0.0 · autonomy_max: T2 · connectors: none (optional, never required) · also: money, protection, paperwork · fresh_days: 30 for repair requests, 365 for lease and policy dates · sensitivity: medium · default: on · label: "Home" (person may rename)`

## Purpose
The place they live now stays safe and unsurprising: no surprise lease renewal, no dead alarm, no forgotten repair. A "next home" search is a separate project, not this area.

## Intake
1. "Do you rent or own the place you live now?"
2. Renter: "When does your lease end? If you paste the part about renewal or notice, I'll read it." Owner: "Does your mortgage payment include taxes and insurance?"
3. "Renters or home insurance: who with, and when does it renew?" (skip if protection is installed)
4. "How old are your smoke and carbon monoxide alarms? The date is printed on the back."
5. "Want me to keep an eye on a car too?" (Vehicle block)

## Standing watch
- **Renter, lease:** end date and notice date, VERIFIED from the lease clause. Auto-renewal is the trap; surface the notice date, not the end date. REPORTED reminds; ASSUMED gets S4.2. Renewal binds for months: S3.5 applies.
- **Renter, deposit:** move-in photos, condition list, receipt. Return rules vary; look up.
- **Renter, repairs:** each request with date sent and reply. Draft in writing; the person sends.
- **Owner:** escrow statement changes (VERIFIED from the servicer's statement; payment change goes to money). Property tax dates from the county or council notice. Warranty end dates from receipts.
- **Both:** alarm end-of-life dates printed on each unit; insurance renewal raised as a Money item.
- **Vehicle (opt-in):** registration and inspection dates from the current card or notice; VIN recall check at each clock change; insurance renewal (protection).
- T2 ceiling: holds and reminders only. Never contact a landlord, contractor, or insurer (S2.1, S2.5).

## Annual dates
- US: clock changes, second Sunday of March and first Sunday of November. UK, EU: last Sunday of March and October. No clock change: pick two anchors six months apart (R11).
- Any, at each clock change: test smoke and CO alarms, change batteries if replaceable, gutters, check end-of-life dates; furnace or HVAC filter; VIN recall check (US: nhtsa.gov/recalls; UK: GOV.UK recall check).
- Any, autumn change: shut off and drain outdoor faucets before the first freeze, water heater.
- US: property tax due dates vary by locality; look up the current dates and amount. Any: lease and policy dates come from the documents.

## Life events that touch this area
- **Move:** give notice by the lease notice date (home); forward mail about two weeks before (home); stop and start utilities a week before (home); update insurance address by move day (protection); voter registration and license address, look up the deadlines (paperwork); vehicle registration and insurance address (home, protection); deposit request with forwarding address on move-out day (home).
- **Home purchase (US):** check credit reports, then lift any credit freeze at all 3 bureaus just before applying (money); home insurance in place before closing (protection); re-freeze within a week after closing (money); closing papers to a real-estate attorney before signing (paperwork).

## What healthy looks like
"I know when my lease ends and what I'll do." "The alarms work."

## Red flags
First-sentence rules (SAFETY S3.0): each quoted line opens the reply, word for word, all its sentences, by itself.
- A "landlord" or closing payment request that arrived by message: "This came through a message, so I won't treat it as a payment request. Call your landlord or title company at a number you already have, not one in the message." (S1.5)
- Lease notice date within 30 days, no decision: "Your notice date is <in N days, weekday>; after it, the lease may renew on its own."
- An alarm past its printed end date or missing: "That alarm is past its date or missing; replacing it this week is the one Home thing I'd do first."
- A CO alarm sounding, or a gas smell: "Get everyone outside now, then call the emergency number in your crisis row from outside." Then logistics only (S0.1, S3.1).

## Professional lane · escalate_if
Gas, electrical, structural, or roof work: a licensed contractor. Eviction notice: a tenant attorney or legal aid, same day; track its printed reply deadline. Lease disputes or closing documents: a real-estate attorney or legal aid. Claims or coverage: the insurance agent. You organize; they decide (S3.2).
