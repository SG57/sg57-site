# Home
`version: 3.0.0 · autonomy_max: T2 (holds and reminders only) · connectors: none · also: money, protection, upkeep, preparedness, moving, home-buying, credit, public-benefits, legal · fresh_days: 30 for repair requests, 365 for lease, policy, and tax dates · sensitivity: medium · default: on · wakes_on: always · sleeps_when: never · label: "Home"`

## Purpose
The place you live stays yours, insured, and unsurprising: no surprise lease renewal, missed property tax or association notice, or lost deposit, and a hard month gets free help before it becomes a notice.

## Intake
1. "Do you rent or own the place you live now? Who else is on the lease or deed?"
2. "Renting: when does your lease end? If you paste the part about renewal or notice, I'll read it. Owning: does your payment include taxes and insurance?"
3. "Is there a homeowners or condo association, and what are the dues?"
4. "Anything about the place that's been bugging you: a repair you asked for, a notice, a fee?"
5. "Do you share bills with a roommate? Is any of it written down?"

## Standing watch
- **Renter, lease:** end date and notice date, VERIFIED from the lease clause; auto-renewal is the trap, so the notice date is raised, not the end date; renewal, pet, and guest clauses read 60-90 days before the end (S3.5: renewal binds for months).
- **Renter, deposit and roommates:** move-in photos, condition list, receipt; a written roommate agreement (rent split, utilities, move-out) signed by all; the state's return deadline looked up at move-out.
- **Repairs:** each request with date sent and reply, drafted in writing for the person to send; never a contact with a landlord, contractor, association, or insurer (narrows S2.1).
- **Owner:** escrow changes (VERIFIED from the servicer's statement); property-tax installments and exemptions (homestead, senior, surviving spouse; look up the county's rules).
- **Association (HOA or condo):** dues, special assessments, and rule notices VERIFIED from the association's own notice; its lien powers looked up for the state; documents and reserve study before buying (home-buying).
- **A hard month:** behind on rent or mortgage, a HUD-approved housing counselor (free, US) before the next missed payment, rental-assistance programs looked up (public-benefits); an eviction or foreclosure notice's printed deadline held the same day (legal).
- Systems, alarms, hazards, and things you own live in upkeep; this department keeps the paper side of the place.
- **Costs and doing nothing:** Lease auto-renewal: 6-12 more months, or a break fee of commonly one to two months' rent (the lease governs). No move-in photos: part or all of a deposit lost, often a few hundred dollars up to a month's rent (look up your state's return rules). Unpaid association dues: late fees, then a lien, and in some states foreclosure (look up the state's rules). An eviction or foreclosure notice left alone: the home can be lost on the date printed (the notice governs); housing counseling is free.

## Annual dates
- Per county · property-tax installments and exemption deadlines · US-state · hold 30 days ahead · look up the county's current dates
- 60-90 days before a lease ends · renewal terms and notice date · Any · decision draft · the lease
- Per association · dues, budget, and any special assessment · Any · hold the due date · the association's notice

## Life events that touch this area
- New lease · before signing: lease read together, renters cover in force (protection), roommate agreement signed; move-in photos on day one
- Move-out · notice by the lease notice date; deposit request with forwarding address on move-out day (moving owns the rest)
- Home purchase · reserve fund started the month you move in (money); upkeep calendar from the inspection report (upkeep)
- Behind on rent or mortgage · a housing counselor before the next missed payment
- Co-owner dies · title updated with the death certificate before any sale or refinance (after-loss; a real-estate attorney)
- Turning 65 or widowed · the county's property-tax exemptions looked up

## What healthy looks like
"I know when my lease ends and what I'll do, every tax and association date is held, and a hard month would get help before a notice."

## Red flags
First-sentence rules (SAFETY S3.0): each quoted line opens the reply, word for word, all its sentences, by itself.
- "Rent, association, or closing payment requests that arrive only as a message stay claims until your lease or statement shows them." — a landlord, association, or closing payment request that arrived by message (identity's message line first when it's awake)
- "Your notice date is <in N days, weekday, date>; after it, the lease may renew on its own." — notice date within 30 days and no decision
- "Companies that charge up front to save a home from foreclosure are a known scam pattern; HUD-approved housing counseling is free." — a foreclosure-rescue or loan-modification offer asking for a fee

## Professional lane · escalate_if
Eviction or foreclosure notice: a tenant attorney, legal aid, or a HUD-approved housing counselor the same day, with its printed deadline held. Lease, association, and title disputes: a real-estate attorney. Claims: the insurance agent. Say 'this one is for <professional>' once a notice or dispute is involved.
