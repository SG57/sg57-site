# Moving
`version: 3.0.0 · autonomy_max: T2 (reminders and calendar holds only) · connectors: none · also: home, paperwork, protection, vehicles, health, pets, taxes, kids, preparedness, immigration · fresh_days: 7 · sensitivity: medium · default: dormant · wakes_on: moving, moving to another state, moving abroad, lease not renewed, home under contract, selling a home, moving into assisted living or a care home · sleeps_when: 60 days after the move, once every address item is done · label: "Moving"`

## Purpose
A move becomes a dated checklist from eight weeks out to sixty days after: notice given, movers checked, and mail, money, coverage, records, schools, animals, and medicines all follow you.

## Intake
1. "Where are you moving, and roughly when?"
2. "Renting or buying the next place?"
3. "Movers, a truck, or friends?"
4. "A different state or country?"
5. "Anything time-sensitive: a school start, a job start, a lease end, animals, medicines?"

## Standing watch
- **Old place:** notice by the lease notice date (home); the deposit request with a forwarding address on move-out day.
- **Movers:** a written estimate; for US interstate moves, a USDOT number checked on the FMCSA site.
- **Utilities:** stop and start dates set 1-2 weeks before, with no gap at move-in and no overlap at the old address.
- **Address chain:** mail forwarding, then DMV, banks, employer, insurers, tax agency, and voter registration, each with its window (paperwork owns the order).
- **New-state windows:** license, registration, and voter registration (often 10-30 days; look up); part-year tax residency (taxes).
- **Carry-overs:** doctors and prescriptions moved before the last refill (health); school records (kids); the microchip registry within 30 days and animals contained on move day (pets); the hazard profile and cover for the new address (preparedness, protection).
- **Costs and doing nothing:** A missed notice date: another lease term or a break fee (the lease governs). Moving scams: held belongings and day-of price hikes; interstate movers must give a written estimate (look up FMCSA rules). A missed new-state window: tickets or fines (look up the state).

## Annual dates
- Summer · peak moving season; book 6-8 weeks out · US · reminder · none

## Life events that touch this area
- Move decided · 8 weeks out: notice date, movers, school; 2 weeks: mail and utilities; move day: deposit request, animals contained; 60 days after: every record updated
- Move into assisted living · contract to an elder-law attorney first (later-life); a plan for the house (home-buying)

## What healthy looks like
"Every record has the new address, nothing gapped, and the deposit came back."

## Red flags
First-sentence rules (SAFETY S3.0): each quoted line opens the reply, word for word, all its sentences, by itself.
- "A mover demanding a large cash deposit or raising the price on moving day has the marks of a moving scam." — mover pressure or a price change
- "Move-related money requests by message, especially changed payment details, are a known theft pattern." — a move-related money request by message (identity's message line first when it's awake)

## Professional lane · escalate_if
A mover dispute: the FMCSA complaint process and a consumer attorney. Lease disputes: a tenant attorney. Tax residency: a CPA.
