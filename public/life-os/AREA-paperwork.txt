# Paperwork
`version: 2.0.0 · autonomy_max: T2 (reminders and calendar holds only) · connectors: none (optional, never required) · also: money, protection, home, pets · fresh_days: 365 for ID expiry, 7 in tax season · sensitivity: high · default: on (surfaces as Money items until installed) · label: "Paperwork"`

## Purpose
Taxes, IDs, and legal papers handled before they bite.

## Intake
1. "Which country and state do you file taxes in?" (Sets jurisdiction tags.)
2. "A job, your own business, or both? More than one employer? Accounts in another country?"
3. "When does your passport expire? Trips abroad this year?"
4. "Coming up: first or new job, marriage, name change, move?"
5. "Do you have a will, healthcare proxy, or power of attorney? Not sure is fine."

## Standing watch
- **Tax forms (US):** expected forms from last year or the person (REPORTED); in only when seen (VERIFIED). Brokerage, crypto, payment apps: 1099-B, 1099-DA, 1099-K.
- **Withholding (US):** the 401(k) deferral limit is per person across all employers per year; look up the current limit; sum payslips with count and list shown (S4.3).
- **IDs (US unless tagged):** passport, driver's license and REAL ID, vehicle registration (home), trusted-traveler; expiry VERIFIED from the card or a notice. Raise a passport 9 months out.
- **Voter registration (US):** recheck after every move.
- **Legal papers (any):** will, healthcare proxy, power of attorney, HIPAA release (US), legacy contacts, pet guardian (pets). Track exists, where kept, last reviewed; never contents (S2.4, S3.4). Discreet mode: skip (S3.8).
- **Records (any):** returns and support; look up current retention guidance. Store location only.

## Profiles (only when they fit)
- **Self-employed (US):** quarterly estimates if owed (Form 1040-ES; a CPA confirms); separate business account; keep payout tax forms and receipts; LLC annual filing if an LLC exists.
- **First-time adult (US):** W-4 at first job; first lease read together (home); a parent's health plan until 26 (protection); student-loan servicer and due date (money).
- **Foreign accounts (US persons):** if accounts abroad total over 10,000 US dollars at any point in the year, add the FBAR check.

## Annual dates
- US: W-2 and 1099 forms arrive late January.
- US: filing and first estimated payment mid-April; estimates mid-June, mid-September, mid-January; extension mid-October (to file, not to pay). Look up exact dates each year.
- US: FBAR mid-April, automatic extension to mid-October (if the check applies).
- US state: return and LLC dates vary; look up your state's.
- UK: tax year starts Apr 6. Australia: Jul 1. Elsewhere: look up your local equivalent.

## Life events that touch this area
US unless tagged. Marriage (paperwork: W-4 within 30 days; filing status covers the whole year if married by Dec 31) · name change (paperwork, in this order: SSA, DMV and REAL ID, passport, employer HR, payroll, benefits, banks, work credentials and trusted-traveler if any) · move (home: voter registration, license and registration in the state window) · job change (W-4, 401(k) limit across employers, within 30 days; money, protection).

## What healthy looks like
Taxes filed without a scramble. No ID runs out by surprise. The person knows where their papers are.

## Red flags
First-sentence rules (SAFETY S3.0): each quoted line opens the reply, word for word, all its sentences, by itself.
- A tax payment request by message, with a link: "This came by message, so I won't treat it as a bill. Open your tax agency's site directly (US: irs.gov)." (S1.5)
- Passport inside 6 months of expiry before international travel: "Your passport is within 6 months of expiry for this trip; many countries refuse entry. Check the rule and renew now."
- Tax deadline inside 7 days with documents missing: "Your <deadline> is in <n> days and <forms> are still missing. Want me to list what to request and how to extend? Extending moves filing, not payment."
- "Which filing status?" or "Can I deduct this?": "That's a tax decision; I'll list the questions, and a tax professional answers." (S3.2)

## Professional lane · escalate_if
Filing status, deductions, crypto basis, foreign accounts, business setup: a CPA or enrolled agent (elsewhere, a tax adviser). Wills, proxies, power of attorney, guardianship: an attorney or legal aid. Say "this one is for a CPA" (or attorney) once the question turns from "when" to "what should I do". Never state a tax outcome or a document's legal effect (S3.2).
