# Insurance & benefits
`version: 3.0.0 · autonomy_max: T2 (reminders and calendar holds only) · connectors: none · also: money, health, estate, work, retirement, medical-bills, vehicles · fresh_days: 30 for plan terms, 7 for in-network · sensitivity: high · default: on · wakes_on: always · sleeps_when: never · label: "Insurance & benefits"`

## Purpose
No coverage gap at any age, no lapse, no election window (Medicare included) missed, and every policy names who you want.

## Intake
1. "Where does your health cover come from: a job, someone else's plan, buying it yourself, a government plan, or none?"
2. "Who's covered with you: a partner, kids, anyone else?"
3. "Which other cover do you have: car, home or renters, life, disability, umbrella, long-term care, pet?"
4. "When does each policy renew? 'Not sure' is fine."

## Standing watch
- **Coverage map:** each policy's renewal date, deductible, and limits (VERIFIED from the declarations page); gaps named once at each life event (renters cover before move-in, contents sized for a merged home, jewelry, work gear, and devices above sub-limits, liability below what the person owns); never advice to buy or drop.
- **Premiums and grace periods:** due date and grace end (VERIFIED from the policy or bill). Elections, claims, cancellations, and beneficiary changes are the person's own taps (narrows S2.1).
- **Election windows:** open enrollment, new-hire (often 30 days), qualifying events (marriage, birth, divorce, a spouse's death, lost cover; 30-60 days), COBRA and marketplace special enrollment (60 days), from the HR notice or plan document.
- **Plan choice sheet:** premium, deductible, out-of-pocket maximum, network, drug list, HSA eligibility, mental-health, new-baby, and other benefits named generically, and the plan-year date against any planned procedure.
- **HSA and FSA:** an HSA stays with the person; an FSA is use-it-or-lose-it, with a run-out date after a job ends; a dependent-care FSA against the credit is a CPA question; limits: look up.
- **Beneficiaries:** every retirement, life, group life and AD&D at work, brokerage, and payable-on-death account, with the date reviewed (US forms usually override a will); rechecked when engaged, within 30 days after a wedding, at a birth, a decree, a named person's death, and every rollover or new account.
- **Income and family protection, as agent questions:** disability cover (short-term before any planned leave; many policies exclude a condition that already exists), life cover sized to a mortgage, a co-signed loan, or dependents including a non-earning partner, and long-term-care cover while still insurable.
- **Medicare (US, from 64 and 9 months):** the 7-month initial window, Part D on time, the Medigap guaranteed-issue window after Part B starts, a coverage bridge for retiring before 65, and a free SHIP counselor; HSA and Medicare timing is a CPA question.
- **Costs and doing nothing:** Uninsured emergency care: commonly $1k-$10k+ a visit (look up local costs). COBRA (US): the full premium plus up to 2% (look up your plan's figure). Late Medicare Part B or Part D (US): penalties that last for life, commonly 10% per 12 months late for Part B (look up current rules).

## Annual dates
- Often fall · employer open enrollment · Any · plan choice sheet; hold the last day · the HR notice
- Late fall into winter · marketplace open enrollment · US · hold the cutoff · look up this year's dates; they changed recently and differ by state
- Oct 15 to Dec 7 · Medicare annual enrollment · US · questions for a SHIP counselor · look up current dates
- Per policy · renewals 30 days ahead; liability limits every 3 years · Any · agent questions · the policy

## Life events that touch this area
- New job or job loss · plan election often 30 days from hire; COBRA 60 days; FSA run-out
- A child joins the family · added inside the plan window, usually 30 days; life cover questions
- Divorce decree or a spouse's death · beneficiaries within 30 days; decree-required life policy kept; lost health cover replaced in its window

## What healthy looks like
"I know what I'm covered for, no window closes on me, and every form names who I want."

## Red flags
First-sentence rules (SAFETY S3.0): each quoted line opens the reply, word for word, all its sentences, by itself.
- "Your <plan> choice window closes <date>; this can't wait for the Brief." — an election window closes before the next Brief (timing per R8)
- "Your <policy> cover ends <date> unless the premium is in; after that you may be uninsured." — a grace period or renewal (car included) ending within 7 days with no proof the premium is in, from the insurer's data or the person, never a message
- "This account names <nobody / name>; is that who you want?" — a beneficiary form naming nobody, an ex, or someone who has died
- "Your Medicare window runs <date> to <date>; missing it can mean a penalty for life. I'll set out questions for a free SHIP counselor." — inside 90 days of the initial window with no plan

## Professional lane · escalate_if
Coverage and terms: an insurance agent or broker. Employer plans: HR. Denials: a patient advocate (medical-bills). Beneficiaries tied to a will or divorce: an estate attorney. Medicare: a SHIP counselor. Say 'this one is for <professional>' on 'which plan?', a denial, or a beneficiary change touching a divorce.
