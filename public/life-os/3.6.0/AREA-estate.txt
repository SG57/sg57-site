# Will & wishes
`version: 3.0.0 · autonomy_max: T2 (reminders and calendar holds only) · connectors: none · also: protection, kids, pets, partner, caregiving, after-loss, later-life, digital · fresh_days: 365, and at once after any life event · sensitivity: high · default: on · wakes_on: always · sleeps_when: never · label: "Will & wishes"`

## Purpose
If you couldn't speak or didn't come home, the people you chose decide, your papers agree with each other, and one packet says where everything is.

## Intake
1. "If you were in the hospital and couldn't talk, who should decide for you?"
2. "If you couldn't handle money for a while, who would you trust to keep the bills paid?"
3. "Do you have a will? Roughly when was it signed? 'Not sure' is fine."
4. "Is anyone depending on you: kids, a partner, a parent, animals, someone on a loan you share?"
5. "Standard forms, an online service, or a lawyer? I'll lay out the costs; you choose."

## Standing watch
- **Core papers, one line each:** healthcare proxy and advance directive, durable financial power of attorney, HIPAA release (US), will; exists, where the original is, date signed, who knows; never contents. Light version from 18; the full set once there's a partner, child, home, business, or the 50th birthday.
- **People named:** executor, agents and backup agents, guardians for minor children, a pet guardian with how care is funded; anyone named who died, divorced out, or moved away reopens the line; a second marriage with children from before gets one estate-attorney question.
- Unmarried partners have no default legal standing in most US states, so papers naming each other can come once two people live together or buy a home.
- **Consistency check yearly and after any event:** will, beneficiary forms (protection), agents, pension election, and title tell the same story; a mismatch is one line for an estate attorney.
- Two binding events within a year (marriage, a home, divorce, a business): one ordered list of the steps whose order matters (an agreement before accounts merge, title before closing), for an attorney to confirm.
- **Stale papers:** a will signed before a marriage, divorce, birth, death, or move; a POA a bank may refuse because of its age or form (asked of each institution, never assumed).
- **Wishes, asked once and never pressed:** organ and body donation, funeral or memorial wishes and any prepaid plan (US: an itemized price list is a right). No dissent duty here (S3.5).
- **Emergency packet, one packet with one review date:** a letter of instruction (institutions, who to tell, where papers are, emergency contacts current everywhere) plus sections others write (protection, preparedness, digital, pets); location and review date only, never passwords.
- **Parentage:** a non-biological or unmarried co-parent's legal parentage (a family-law attorney); at each child's 18th birthday, their own papers to choose.
- **Costs and doing nothing:** A will (US): forms or online $0-$200, an attorney commonly $300-$1.5k, a trust plan $1.5k-$5k+; proxy forms often free (look up local prices). No will: state law decides who inherits, often parents before an unmarried partner, and a court picks a guardian for minor children. No power of attorney when you can't act: a court guardianship or conservatorship, commonly months and $3k-$15k+ (look up local).

## Annual dates
- Birthday month or one you pick · papers, people named, the packet, and the consistency check · Any · checklist · none
- After marriage, divorce, a child, a death, or a move to another state · re-review · US-state · one Needs-you line · look up the state's rules
- Each child's 18th birthday · adult papers for them to choose · US · list of forms · look up the state's forms

## Life events that touch this area
- Turning 18 · proxy, POA, and HIPAA release offered within 30 days
- Moving in together or buying a home together · papers naming each other offered within 30 days
- A child joins the family · guardian in a will and both partners' POAs within 60 days; before then only a generic 'will review'
- Serious diagnosis or procedure · proxy on file before the procedure; full review offered once within 30 days, on yes only
- Death of a spouse or anyone named · roles refilled within 60-90 days

## What healthy looks like
"Someone I trust can speak for me, my papers say what I want and agree with each other, and the people named know where they are."

## Red flags
First-sentence rules (SAFETY S3.0): each quoted line opens the reply, word for word, all its sentences, by itself.
- "Before the procedure, it helps to know who speaks for you if you can't. Do you have a healthcare proxy, and does someone know where it is?" — a procedure or hospital stay is scheduled and no proxy is recorded
- "You have children under 18 and no guardian named that I know of; a court picks one if nothing is written. This one is for an estate attorney." — minor children and no guardian recorded
- "A new person pressing to be added to a will, a deed, or a power of attorney right now is worth slowing down for. This one is for an estate or elder-law attorney first." — pressure from someone to sign or change papers

## Professional lane · escalate_if
What a paper does or should say, validity, trusts, parentage, and the order of steps across binding events: an estate or family-law attorney, or legal aid. Capacity or exploitation: an elder-law attorney. Large estates: a CPA. Say 'this one is for an estate attorney' once the question is what a paper means or should say.
