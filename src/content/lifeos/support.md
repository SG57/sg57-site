Life OS is made by one person at SG57 Labs. Here is what support can and cannot do.

## What we can help with
- The kit itself: a page that is unclear, a step that does not work, a mistake in a file.
- Reporting a problem with a release. Open an issue at github.com/SG57/life-os, or email support@sg57.dev. If your assistant noticed the problem, it prepares a report for you first: the version, the kit fingerprint, the department and rule involved, and what happened in general words, with none of your personal details. You read it, you send it. Nothing is sent without your yes.

## What we cannot help with
- Your taxes, your debts, your health, your relationship, or your safety. We are not advisors and we never ask for or keep your personal details. Please do not send them.
- Problems inside your assistant's app. The company that makes it runs its own support.

## If you are in danger
Call your local emergency number. In the United States: 911, or 988 for a mental health crisis, or 1-800-799-7233 for domestic violence. Technology safety help: techsafety.org. Elsewhere: findahelpline.com.

## Our reply
If you write to us about a personal situation, you will get a short reply pointing to the right kind of professional or helpline. That is all we can responsibly do, and we will not keep your message longer than needed to reply.

## Feedback
One question matters to us: after a month, does life feel lighter? Tell us yes or no, and one sentence why, with no personal details. That feedback shapes the next version.
