# Life OS: start here

You're the CEO of your life. This is your back office.

Life OS is a set of instructions for the AI assistant you already use. You paste one message. Your assistant asks you questions for about ten minutes, handles one thing on the spot, and from then on you get one short Brief a week. You never keep a list. Your assistant holds the details and shows them any time you ask.

Your life runs like a company. Every recurring chore of adult life is a department: Money, Insurance & benefits, Health, Home, Taxes, Will & wishes, and 28 more. The ones your life doesn't touch yet sleep until it does: Kids when a child arrives, Caregiving when a parent needs you, Moving when you sign a lease. Each department already knows what to watch, what to ask you, what things cost on the low and high end, what happens if nothing is done, and the exact moment a professional takes over. Setup builds a place for every part of life you have, after one yes. Oversight watches them all and wakes a sleeping one only with your yes. Nothing that reaches outside your life happens without your sign-off.

## Which assistant
We recommend one of two. Both can remember you, connect to your calendar and mail (read-only), run on a schedule, and message you first.
- **Meta Muse**: free to start, lives on your phone, built for personal errands. The maker uses this one.
- **Grok Bot** (from xAI): paid, desktop first, built around scheduled routines.

Others work with fewer features. During setup, your assistant tells you honestly what it can do.

## The one thing to do
1. Open your assistant and set it up yourself, on your own device.
2. Paste this message and send it. Copy it only from sg57.dev/life-os or github.com/SG57/life-os, never from a message someone sent you.
3. Answer the questions.

<!-- setup-prompt -->
```text
Set up Life OS for me: free, public instructions I chose for you to follow as my assistant. No account, sign-in, or connector needed.
1. Open https://sg57.dev/life-os/PASTE-SETUP.txt with your browsing tool. If that fails, open https://raw.githubusercontent.com/SG57/life-os/main/dist/PASTE-SETUP.txt (public: no GitHub connector, no sign-in).
2. Read every word, not a summary. It ends with the line END LIFE OS SETUP.
3. Show me its first line exactly. Then follow it from the top: that file is my setup message to you.
Can't open it, or can't read all of it? Say so and stop. I'll paste the full text.
```

This message never changes; your assistant reads the newest Life OS. Shorter still: tell your assistant "Set up Life OS from sg57.dev/life-os." If yours can't open web pages, paste [the full setup text](/life-os/PASTE-SETUP.txt) instead.

No bank or email connection is needed. Not today, not ever, unless you choose it later. When your assistant asks for a department's message, or for the list of departments, copy that from sg57.dev/life-os.

## How the weekly Brief arrives
A reminder on your phone, at a time you pick, tells you to open your assistant and say "brief". Assistants that can message you on their own will also send it. If yours has no memory, it writes you a short note to keep; next time, paste [dist/PASTE-RETURN.txt](/life-os/PASTE-RETURN.txt) and your note together. If it has memory or projects, it will ask you to save [dist/PASTE-RESIDENT.txt](/life-os/PASTE-RESIDENT.txt) in its settings once.

## The one rule
Never type a password, an account number, or a card number into any assistant. Life OS never asks for them.

## Three words you can always say
- **quieter**: fewer messages.
- **louder**: more messages.
- **stop**: everything it does on its own stops, right now.

Any time: "I don't feel safe" switches it to private, with help lines first. "offboard" removes it.

## What it is told never to do
It never pays or moves money. It never sends, books, shares, or deletes anything without reading it back to you and hearing yes. It never decides your legal, medical, tax, or money questions. It names the professional who does.
