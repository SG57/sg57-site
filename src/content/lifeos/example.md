This example is fictional and written to follow the setup script; it is not a recorded chat.

Maya copied the setup message from sg57.dev/life-os and pasted it into a free chat assistant with no memory and no connections.

## Setting up

**Assistant:** "Nothing gets sent, booked, or shared without reading it back to you first. I never pay or move money; that stays with you. No bank or email connection is needed, today or ever. Say 'stop' at any time and everything I do on my own stops. Say 'show me what you see' and I'll show you exactly what I'm holding. My scam warnings never contain a link or a phone number; if one does, it was copied from a message, so ignore it."

**Maya:** Okay.

**Assistant:** Honestly: I can't make reminders here; your phone's calendar does that. I won't remember this chat next week unless you paste your note back. I can't read your email, calendar, or bank account.

**Maya:** Fine, let's go.

**Assistant:** Which country, and which state or province?

**Maya:** United States. Ohio.

**Assistant:** "In case you ever need them: in an emergency, 911; crisis line, 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: call or text 988; chat at chat.988lifeline.org; 24/7; English and Spanish. Say 'crisis lines' any time to hear them." What's your home time zone? And I can't see a clock I trust here — what's today's date?

**Maya:** Eastern. Sunday, October 18, 2026.

**Assistant:** Today is Sunday, 2026-10-18, going by what you told me.

**Assistant:** One private question, for your safety: does anyone else know your phone passcode or your email password, get your account's security emails, or use this assistant account?

**Maya:** No, just me.

**Assistant:** Should I speak up first when I notice something, or wait to be asked? Business words, or plain? Any hours I should never message you, and which day should stay quiet?

**Maya:** Speak up, plain. Nothing before 8am or after 10pm; Mondays stay quiet, my day off.

**Assistant:** What's been nagging you? Messy is fine. Say it all; I'll sort it.

**Maya:** Kitchen faucet's leaked under the sink a week, landlord's done nothing. Car payment's $214, due Thursday. My cat Pickle needs the vet. My schedule changes every week. And I don't know if I've got $500 saved if something broke.

**Assistant:** 5 things: two about money, one about the leak, one about Pickle's vet visit, one about your schedule. The leak costs most the longer it sits. Here's a text, ready: "Hi, it's Maya. My kitchen faucet has been leaking under the sink for about a week. Could someone take a look this week? Thanks." Change it, or ready?

**Maya:** Good as is. *(sends it)* Sent.

**Assistant:** Handled. What time is it there right now?

**Maya:** 7:40pm.

**Assistant:** Add to your phone: "Life OS test" at 7:50 pm, Eastern.

**Maya:** Added.

*(Ten minutes later.)*

**Assistant:** Did it go off?

**Maya:** Yes.

**Assistant:** I'd track two things, plus Good Life, which always stays on: money, and home (the leak and the landlord).

**Maya:** Those two.

**Assistant:** Please paste the area message for money from https://sg57.dev/life-os.

**Maya:** *(pastes it)*

**Assistant:** Matches my kit — using it. Rent or mortgage, and when is it due?

**Maya:** Rent, $900, due the 1st.

**Assistant:** Any debt that charges interest? Just the kind and rough size is fine.

**Maya:** Just the car loan, about $9,000 left.

*(The assistant asks the other three money questions as written. To "If a surprise cost $500 this week, is that covered?" Maya says, "I don't know.")*

**Assistant:** Please paste the area message for home from https://sg57.dev/life-os.

**Maya:** *(pastes it)*

**Assistant:** Matches my kit — using it. Do you rent or own the place you live now?

**Maya:** Rent.

*(The assistant asks the other home questions as written.)*

**Assistant:** What are the 3 things that matter most right now? What does a good week look like? What are you choosing to be mediocre at this season?

**Maya:** Keeping the car running, not falling behind on rent, and getting Pickle to the vet. A good week: all shifts covered, nothing bounces, one night to myself. The apartment can look messy.

**Assistant:** Where should I never be involved? Some people pick: intimacy, what you and a partner say to each other, friendships as numbers, faith, grief, play.

**Maya:** My love life, and don't turn my friends into a number.

**Assistant:** What's the one thing that's yours alone, that I protect time for?

**Maya:** Sunday morning roller derby practice.

**Assistant:** I'll keep it in mind. When is a reliable quiet moment in your week?

**Maya:** Sunday nights, after I feed Pickle, around 8.

**Assistant:** Add this one too, repeating every Sunday at 8 pm, Eastern: "Life OS brief: open your assistant and say 'brief'." Or say 'brief' whenever you're ready.

**Maya:** Added.

**Assistant:** 5 items, 1 handled, 0 connections, areas: money, home, Good Life. Help lines: United States. Style: speak up, plain. Brief: Sunday 8pm.
