> Faucet. Car payment Thursday. The cat's vet. Do I have $500 saved?

That's the list most of us carry in our heads. I wanted it out of mine.

## What changed

AI chat used to answer and forget. The newest assistants remember you, can check your calendar if you let them, and can message you first. That last part is the big one: an assistant that can reach out can run your life's back office.

## What Life OS is

A free set of instructions you paste into the AI app you already use. I use Meta Muse. Grok Bot is the other one I recommend; others work with fewer features.

- **Twenty minutes** of plain questions. It handles one thing on the spot.
- **One short Brief a week**: what got done, at most three things that need you, one thing to look forward to.
- **You never keep a list.**

It never pays or moves money, never sends anything without reading it back and hearing your yes, and never decides legal, medical, tax, or money questions.

## Honest status

Version 2.0.0 is a release candidate: the safety tests are written but not yet run on a real assistant. I'm trying it on my own life first. Set it up yourself, on your own device, and copy it only from the official page.

<summary>The longer story: why I built it</summary>

Adult life has a back office. Bills, renewals, the dentist you keep meaning to book, the lease clause you never read, the benefits window that quietly closes. None of it is hard. All of it is *remembering*, and remembering is the part that wears you down.

Companies solved this long ago. They have departments. Someone notices, someone plans, someone does, and the CEO reads a one-page summary. Most of us run our lives with no departments at all: one tired person holding everything in their head, usually at 11pm. I wanted the department without the company.

Version 1 was four files my assistant wrote for me. I handed it to a review process that was deliberately unkind: seventeen independent lenses found 65 gaps, six architectures competed on a frozen scorecard, and the winner was tested by handing it blind to assistants and seeing what they did. Every failure traced back to wording I could fix, and I fixed it, twice.

Why "back office" and not "life coach"? Because the point is to work less and get more, not to be optimized. No streaks, no scores, no goals attached to fun, rest, or friends. It asks where it should never be involved. Good Life is the one area that is always on: there is always one thing to look forward to.
